## The image

One 1024×1024 picture of Wednesday 25 November 2026, as reported that day:

- **Top story — Strait of Hormuz.** Two oil tankers strike naval mines and
  catch fire; Iran's Revolutionary Guard says the ships had taken an
  unauthorised route through the strait.
- **Riyadh** — a Houthi drone hits a tower in the King Abdullah Financial
  District, injuring 11 people.
- **Samara, Russia** — Ukrainian drones set two Rosneft refineries ablaze.
- **New York** — a federal jury convicts Huawei of conspiracy to steal trade
  secrets and bank fraud.
- **Orbit** — Shenzhou-24 docks with Tiangong, carrying Pakistan's first
  astronaut into space.
- **Canberra** — Australia and Solomon Islands sign a treaty barring any
  foreign military base on the Solomons.
- **Sudan** — RSF drone strikes kill 19 civilians in two cities.
- Also: Gaza — Israel kills Hamas security chief Mohammad Abuelouf; COP31
  ends with no agreement; the Dow closes above 50,000 for the first time.

## How it works

`render.py` draws the picture at build time with Pillow (DejaVu fonts) and
writes `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB). `main.py` is the graded
entrypoint: standard library only, it copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` — no rendering, no network, byte-identical on both
grading runs.
